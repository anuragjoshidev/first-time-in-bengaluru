# Contributing

Two ways to help:

1. **Comment on an Issue** — fastest, no setup. Just reply with what you know.
2. **Open a PR** — fork this repo, add one line to the matching file in
   `/asks`, submit. Smallest possible diff, on purpose.

## reward

Pick your treat:

- a cup of **chino**
- **garam chai** ki pyaali
- **one pint please**

Budget is finite. Early reviewers get covered. Late ones still get the thanks.
